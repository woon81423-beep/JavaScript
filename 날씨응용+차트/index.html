CLEARING THE SKIES: SEOUL FACES COOL AUTUMN GUSTS

Raindrops do not fall in classic teardrop shapes as they descend through the atmosphere. While a small droplet starts out roughly spherical due to surface tension, the upward rush of air flattens its bottom into a shape more like a hamburger bun. Only when a drop grows large enough to split apart does it briefly trail a thin, tapered wake before breaking into smaller pieces.
Thunder never strikes without lightning, yet the two are simply different manifestations of the same electrical discharge. Lightning heats the surrounding air in a fraction of a second to temperatures hotter than the surface of the sun. That sudden thermal expansion sends out a shockwave that we hear as thunder, traveling far more slowly than the flash of light that caused it./li>
A rainbow is not a physical arch anchored in a specific spot on the horizon, but an optical illusion shaped by the angle of sunlight. Each observer sees their own personal rainbow formed by light refracting, reflecting, and dispersing inside countless suspended water droplets. Because the geometry depends on where you stand, moving forward or sideways shifts the entire arc along with you.
